# Mathematical Cymatics & Harmonic Coupling Kiro Power

2. **Fibonacci Recurrence (`F_n / F_{n-1}`)**:
   - Consecutive ratios (`1/1, 2/1, 3/2, 5/3, 8/5, 13/8, 21/13`) converge toward `φ`, generating phyllotaxis divergence angles (`137.508°`), Fibonacci nodal petals, and syncopated rhythmic gates.
3. **Golden Ratio (`φ = 1.6180339887`)**:
   - Irrational modulator ratios (`f_m = f_c · φ`) prevent periodic phase cancellation, yielding crystalline bell spectra and self-similar logarithmic spirals (`r(θ) = r_0 · φ^(2θ/π)`).
4. **Pythagorean Just Intonation (`3:2 & 4:3`)**:
   - Pure fifths (`3:2`) and fourths (`4:3`) generated via `(3/2)^k mod 2` produce zero-beating acoustic intervals and closed rational cymatic knots.
